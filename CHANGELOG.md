# Changelog

What changed in each release, newest first. Pushing a `vX.Y.Z` tag releases the section headed
`## [X.Y.Z]`: its notes become the GitHub release's and the update window's, and the release
workflow stops if the section is missing. Write under Unreleased as you go; `docs/release.md`
has the rest.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Updates arrive in the background: **Yongkang → Restart to Update** installs one, and quitting
  installs it anyway. **Check for Updates…** asks straight away.
- **Sign In with GitHub…** in the Yongkang menu. It is optional; nothing in the app needs it.
- Usage analytics: which parts of the app are used, tied to your account once you sign in.
  **Share Usage Analytics** in the Yongkang menu turns it off. No path, name, prompt or file's
  contents is ever sent.
